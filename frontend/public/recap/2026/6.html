How the chances are figured

Each starter's score is drawn 10,000 times from real NFL games played with a similar betting line and roof, scored with Shit Ball's rules. A kept bye player, a starter ruled out, or a starter with no NFL team counts as a certain -8, and a game already played keeps its real points.

Tested on 189 past games from 2025 and 2026: the favorite won 52%, with a Brier score of 0.248 against a coin flip's 0.250 (lower is better). That edge over a coin flip is small, so read every number as a lean.
